the specification

The implementable half of the design system: every token, every rule, and all fourteen primitives specified to the level a developer can build from without asking a second question.

Platform Responsive web · React Accessibility WCAG 2.2 Level AA Units rem, with argued px constants Themes light · dark Tiers four, from 320px

How to read this

The kernel is the authority. tokens.css is the only place a colour, a type step, a spacing unit, a duration, a layer or a breakpoint is declared. This document restates those values for reading; where the two disagree, the kernel wins. If a component needs a value that is not in the kernel, that is a finding — add it there, or state why it is local. It is never a local invention.

Every token has exactly one job, and the job is what you pick by. Not the look. A step used outside its job is a defect even when it looks right, because the next reader will copy the look and lose the meaning.

Each primitive's specification below has a 🛑 Never row. Those are not style preferences. Each one names a defect that was built, measured and removed, and the row exists so it is not rebuilt.

The companion reference sheet draws every primitive live, with the demonstrations this document describes. Read them together: this one is the contract, that one is the evidence.

1 · Visual identity

The mark

  • CompositionWordmark "Reconcile" in the sans face at weight 700, tracking −.025em, followed by a square badge carrying "AI" on a 90° cyan→green gradient.
  • --badge-gradientlinear-gradient(90deg, #50c6e9, #72c34e). The badge.
  • --badge-gradient-glyphlinear-gradient(135deg, same stops). The standalone glyph. Two tokens and not one: the badge and the glyph run at different angles, and a single token would force one back inline.
  • --badge-radius0, under R2. The brand sheet's own corner is .09em; this token is the single revert point.
  • Clear spaceThe badge's own width on every side. Expressed in em so it travels with the mark at any size.
  • Minimum size16px of badge. Below that the two letterforms in the gradient stop resolving, and the mark should be the glyph alone.
  • On an ink ground.logo--invert switches the wordmark to --on-ink. The badge gradient is unchanged — it holds against both grounds.
🛑 These are the only two gradients in the system. A gradient that is not the mark is a finding: a gradient on a surface is an elevation claim, and there is no elevation scale. The brief's "subtle gradients" is declined here rather than quietly ignored. The luminous touch is the one apparent exception and is not one — a radial source with falloff is light, and light carries no elevation claim.

Why the accent is warm, and outside the brand family. A second green would be read as a second agent state, which is the exact confusion the agent rule exists to prevent. The luminous touch

Apply .lit to anything the machine is working on right now. A radial source at 18%/0% with falloff to nothing by 74% — never a flat fill, never a border alone, and never more than one lit thing per view: one light is legible, six lights are wallpaper. Light values: --lit-hot rgba(202,246,65,.34) and --lit-mid rgba(82,198,227,.22), dropping to .26/.16 in the dark theme, where the same stops composite to a genuine glow rather than to a tint.

The light is pale, and that is what stops a lime light reading as an amber status: composited on paper the hot centre is rgb(237,252,190), a 24.3% channel span. .lit--running adds the sweep — one slow pass per 17s with 88% of the cycle at rest.

⚠️ Implementation note that has bitten this system twice. The paint is declared on a doubled class (.lit.lit), and background-color and background-image are declared separately. background: is a shorthand that resets background-image, so any component writing background: var(--x) at single-class specificity erased the light — silently, because nothing else about the element changed. The same trap applies to .nb. Use background-color, always.

2 · Typography

Two faces, and no third

  • --face-sansIBM Plex Sans, system-ui, sans-serif. Carries language: every sentence, label, control and heading.
  • --face-monoIBM Plex Mono, ui-monospace, monospace. Carries figures: anything a person compares against another instance of itself — a figure, a reference, a hash, a key-cap. Always with tabular numerals.

There is no display face. A third family would have to say what it is for, and both jobs are taken. The division between the two is R9, not taste.

Six steps, each arriving with its pairing decided

--s5 does not exist: nothing in the system is between a title and a figure, and minting the step is how a fifth heading level arrives without anybody deciding to add one.

The three small steps do not scale with the viewport, and that is the rule rather than an omission. A 14px body is 14px on a phone and on a 32-inch display, because it is already at the readable floor and a viewport-scaled body text shrinks below it on exactly the device where reading is hardest. Only the three steps above body are fluid.
⚠️ The fluid steps clamp with a rem floor, never a vw floor. A pure vw value ignores the reader's font-size setting entirely, which fails 1.4.4 Resize Text while looking responsive.

Six weights, each with one job

  • --w-display 200--s7 only. Nothing else is ever this light.
  • --w-lede 300The one lede paragraph, and the --s6 figure.
  • --w-body 400Everything that is a sentence.
  • --w-figure 500A monospaced thing that must not look bold — a key-cap, a token name, a reference.
  • --w-emph 600Emphasis, and every uppercase micro label.
  • --w-mark 700The wordmark and the glyph. Nothing else.

🛑 A weight is never used for emphasis outside its job. 500 on a sentence of prose is not "slightly bold" — it is the figure weight appearing where no figure is, and it reads as a rendering accident.

Leading, tracking, measure

Leading: the smallest type gets the loosest. That is the reverse of the instinct and it is the whole rule — a 12.5px note is read in long paragraphs at a size where lines are easy to lose, so it needs more room between them than 14px body does. All three values are unitless multipliers, so a reader's 1.5× override under 1.4.12 Text Spacing multiplies rather than replaces them.

Tracking is paired with the step, not chosen at the call site. Both values are optical corrections for size — 11px uppercase closes up to illegibility without +.1em, and a 96px display opens into gaps without −.035em — which is why neither is available to a step that did not ask for one. In em and not rem: tracking must scale with the type it is tracking.

MeasureValueFor
--measure-text80chBody and note prose, and it is the ceiling. Past about eighty characters the eye loses the start of the next line.
--measure-lede64chThe one lede. Shorter because it is bigger.
--measure-act56chA sentence a person must act on: a route state, a dialog's consequence, an error.

🛑 Never set a text measure in px or rem. A ch measure holds when the step changes; a fixed measure silently becomes 120 characters at --s2 and 50 at --s4. --measure (73.75rem) is a different thing entirely — a page — and the two must never be written in terms of each other.

3 · Space, layer, target

  • --u · 0.25rem4px at a 16px root. Every gap is a multiple, written calc(var(--u)*n) so the multiple stays legible at the call site. The half-unit is the floor — a quarter-unit is a finding. No half-unit token is minted: every site already spells it one way, and a token would create a second spelling.
  • --gutterclamp(1.25rem, .85rem + 2vw, 3.5rem) — 20→56px. The page's own edge.
  • --measure · 73.75rem1180px. A page of content. Does not grow above the ultra tier: a line of text that keeps widening eventually exceeds every text measure. Never rewritten in terms of the entry-tier form column, which is calc(var(--u)*102) and moves for different reasons.
  • --col-min · 15.5rem248px. The floor of an auto-fit grid column.
  • --target-min · 1.5rem24px. The WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8 floor, declared once as a rule so a new control cannot be born under it. In rem, so a reader who raises their font size gets a bigger target — a px floor satisfies the criterion as written and fails the person it was written for.
  • --target · 2rem32px. What the system's controls actually are. 24 is a floor, not a design target — nothing is designed at 24.
  • --z-popover 20A tooltip or a menu — anchored to something.
  • --z-overlay 30A drawer — over the register, never away from it.
  • --z-modal 40A dialog, its scrim, and the toast.
  • --z-skip 99The skip link. Highest, because it is the one control that must be reachable when something else has gone wrong.

The four layers are spaced by ten so a component can sit between two without reopening the scale. The order is the decision rather than the numbers.

Depth, and why there is no elevation scale

A surface separates by rule or by ground. There is no shadow palette and no elevation scale; the two scrims are the only exception, and they exist because a modal must dim what it blocks. --sheet-shadow is not a shadow — it is a hairline under a panel, which is why it is spelled 0 1px 0 var(--rule-strong).

Border weight is deliberately not tokenised. There is no scale, there are three unrelated facts, and all three stay in px because a hairline that scales with the reader's font size becomes a border: 1px is the only edge weight the system has and it never varies; 1.5px occurs once, on a hollow connection dot, as geometric compensation — a hollow 7px dot needs a thicker ring than a solid one to read at the same weight; 2px is emphasis, which is a rule rather than a size: the 1px edge doubled and switched to full --ink, never a hue change.

4 · Motion

  • --springlinear(0, .231 10%, .624 20%, .922 30%, 1.067 40%, 1.094 50%, 1.067 60%, 1.030 70%, 1.005 80%, .995 90%, 1). A real damped spring — ζ 0.6, ωn 18 rad/s — sampled at eleven points and written as an easing function. Overshoots 9% and settles.
  • --t-spring · 420msThe spring's settle time. Anything that moves, on the way in.
  • --t-fade · 120msOpacity and colour, and the way out.
🛑 A leaving element does not bounce. Spring on the way in, plain fade on the way out. The overshoot means "this landed here", and an element on its way off screen has nothing to land on — a bounce on exit reads as a failure to leave. The spring carries the asymmetry the old two-duration scale carried in its numbers, which is why one spring replaces two durations rather than sitting alongside them.
⚠️ --t-fade is not a second spring. An opacity change has no distance to ease across, so a spring on it is mathematically meaningless — the overshoot clamps at 1 and the curve degenerates. It is a geometric necessity, the same category as the 1.5px hollow dot.

Why linear() and not a JS spring. It runs on the compositor as an ordinary CSS transition, needs no runtime, survives the reduced-motion guard, and cannot disagree with itself between two components the way two spring configs in two files would. What it gives up is interruptibility: a JS spring retargets mid-flight from its current velocity, and this one restarts. That trade is why it is one token and not a library, and it is recorded as an open finding rather than as a win.

The ambient tempo rule. Motion measured in seconds means "this is live". Motion measured in milliseconds means "you did that". The two budgets never mix: retiming the sweep to the spring would make it mean "this just happened", and stretching a drawer to 17s would make an overlay claim to be an ongoing process.

⚠️ Two traps in the reduced-motion guard, both load-bearing. * alone does not match pseudo-elements, and both kernel animations run on one — a guard written *{} looks complete, passes review, and stops neither; *,*::before,*::after is required. And .001ms merely makes an infinite animation fast, not still, which is why the sweep and the halo are stopped by their own selectors on top of the universal rule.

5 · Iconography

🛑 There is no icon set, and that is a decision rather than an absence. The system has six glyphs. All six are typographic — set in the text face at the parent's size, never drawn, never an SVG, never a font-icon. The metered mark is not among them: it is the mascot's still form, ruled under R19 and documented with the mark.

An icon set grows by resemblance rather than by decision — the next icon arrives because a shape existed — and under R1 an icon cannot carry state anyway, so every icon the system could add would be decoration on a word that is already doing the work.

7 · The not-built treatment

🛑 Required on any feature a decision refused or left unbuilt. A mockup carrying one of them without this treatment becomes evidence of a shipped control. Three parts, and all three are required:

  • .nb.nbThe hatch. 135° repeating stripe, 6px transparent / 1px --hatch, at alpha .18 — 1.42:1 on both light grounds, and 1.55:1 in the dark theme where the stripe inverts to a light value. It inverts or it disappears: a dark stripe on a dark ground is the not-built defect in its purest form.
  • .nb::afterThe word, from data-nb. Uppercase --s1 in --refused, top right, on a --paper chip with a 1px edge. It survives greyscale printing and does not depend on hue.
  • .nbwhyThe reason — the sentence naming the decision that refused it. A hatch without a reason is half the treatment: the word says that, the reason says which decision, and a reviewer cannot act on the first without the second.
⚠️ Why the alpha is .18 and not .055. At .055 the stripe composited to 1.11:1 on every ground — fainter than --rule at 1.38:1, the faintest line the system deliberately declares — so the hatch sat below the visible floor on the ground it was calibrated for. The reported premise that this was --sheet-specific was exactly inverted: an alpha composite scales the ground rather than displacing it, which is what makes an alpha stripe ground-independent, and the solid stripe the finding implied would have introduced the defect it named.

⚠️ The focus ring is an outline, and this is not cosmetic. It used to be a box-shadow with outline:none and no fallback at specificity (0,1,0), so any component declaring its own box-shadow edge replaced the ring outright and left nothing behind — box-shadow does not accumulate across rules. Eleven files had restated it locally and they had already drifted apart. An outline and a box-shadow are different properties: a component's edge cannot replace the ring at any specificity, in any source order. The failure class is gone, not just its instances. Do not remove outline-offset to tighten the ring — an ink-ground control carrying --on-ink text would take an ink ring at the border box and be invisible.

6 · Filter mechanism — <Filters>

AnatomyFacet list → active-filter chips → clear-all → count line. Each facet is a button.facet with its count in mono; each active filter is a removable .chip.
StatesFacet: rest · hover · aria-pressed="true" (--sheet ground + --w-emph) · focus. Clear-all is aria-disabled when nothing is filtered.
The count lineStates its scope and its denominator. "6 of 14 shown · 32 in the register · 18 held out by the current filter". A filtered count with neither is a number nobody can check.
BehaviourThe facet list is not a design choice — it is the closed set the contract already carries. A facet the query cannot express is drawn as a hole (hatched, with its reason), never invented in the UI: inventing it means filtering a page of rows instead of the register, which is a filter lying about its denominator.
ResponsiveNarrow: facets move into a sheet; the chips stay visible, because what is filtered must never be hidden behind a tap.
🛑 NeverA facet the query cannot express · a count without a denominator · a chip whose removal is not a single target ≥24px.

7 · Table navigation — <Register>

Anatomytable inside a single-axis .scroller; sticky thead on a --paper ground; a sort control in the column head; a load-more control below.
SortCarried by a word, never a glyph. A closed enum of four; the control states the current mode in words and cycles. aria-sort on the column.
LoadProgressive, and no page numbers: the register grows while you read it, so page three is not a stable place.
Sticky headThe only sticky element in the system, and a focused row scrolls clear of it — 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured. A column heading that scrolls away turns every figure into an unlabelled number.
FiguresEvery value and score is mono with tabular numerals, right-aligned. R9.
ResponsiveNarrow: triages to three columns — reference, exception, whose move — folding value and score into the row's second line. It is the one table that reflows rather than scrolling, because it is the one surface a person reads for hours.
🛑 NeverA glyph-only sort affordance · page numbers · a fixed height on a row · two-dimensional scrolling at 320px.

8 · Side sheet / drawer — <Drawer>

Anatomyscrim → panel · .ov-head (title, context, close) → .ov-body (scrolls) → .ov-foot (actions, right-aligned).
SurfaceTHE OVERLAY SURFACE: --paper, 1px --rule-strong on the entry side only, radius 0, no shadow.
GeometryRight edge, full height, min(92%, calc(var(--u)*110)) — 440px at a 16px root. Layer --z-overlay.
MotionIn on --spring at --t-spring. Out on --t-fade.
ARIArole="dialog", aria-modal="false", aria-labelledby the title.
BehaviourDoes not trap focus — the register behind it stays operable, which is the whole point of a drawer. Escape closes; the scrim closes. Closing undoes nothing, because asking is not an act.
ResponsiveNarrow: full width, no left edge — 92% of 360px leaves a 28px strip of register that shows nothing and reads as a mistake. Ultra: becomes an inline third column and stops being an overlay at all.
🛑 NeverA shadow · an edge on more than one side · a focus trap · replacing the register rather than sitting over it · a bounce on the way out.

9 · Modal dialog, scrim, focus trap — <Dialog>

Anatomyscrim at --z-modal → centred panel → head, body, foot. The committing action is the data-commit button: ink ground, --on-ink text, --agent-deep on hover.
Geometrymin(92%, calc(var(--u)*130)), max-height 90%, 1px edge on all four sides.
MotionIn on --spring, rising calc(var(--u)*4). The scrim fades on --t-fade.
ARIArole="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby. The reason field carries aria-invalid and aria-describedby its own message.
BehaviourTraps focus; restores it to the opener. Escape cancels; the scrim does not — a destructive act is not dismissed by a misclick. The commit control is aria-disabled until the required reason is entered.
The reasonProduct machinery, not a courtesy. It is what an SOP amendment would be drafted from, which is why the act cannot commit without it — and why it is entered once and attached to all three affected records (3.3.7 Redundant Entry).
ResponsiveNarrow: full width less the gutter; footer buttons stack full width.
🛑 NeverScrim-click to dismiss · a commit enabled without its reason · a shadow · an autofocus on the committing control.

10 · Tooltip / popover — <Tip>

Anatomy.tw positioning wrapper → the trigger control → .tip panel. The panel is a sibling of the trigger, never a child.
SurfaceTHE TOOLTIP SURFACE: --ink ground, --on-ink text, --on-ink-2 second line, radius 0, no edge — 14.05:1 against paper already separates it.
GeometryAbove the trigger, offset calc(var(--u)*2), left-aligned to it. Max width calc(var(--u)*64). Layer --z-popover.
StatesTwo only: [data-open] absent, or "true". There is no hover state, no loading state and no placement variant.
Motionopacity var(--t-fade) linear. Not the spring.
ARIAPanel is role="tooltip" with an id; the trigger carries aria-describedby. A disabled trigger uses aria-disabled="true", never disabled.
BehaviourThe open state has exactly one writer, and it is the script. Focus opens it; tap opens it; pointerenter may open it only by calling the same function. Escape clears the attribute and stops propagation so an enclosing overlay does not also close.
Two guardsA tap focuses then clicks — without a pointer guard the pair opens on focus and closes on click, and the tap user sees nothing. A browser restores focus on reload — without an interaction guard the panel opens before anyone has touched the page.
ResponsiveUnchanged. Narrow: opened by tap, dismissed by tap-away or Escape.
🛑 Neverdisplay:none · visibility:hidden · any CSS selector keyed on :hover, :focus-visible or :focus-within · hover as the only way in · a tooltip carrying information stated nowhere else.
⚠️ Why no selector may open it. A script can clear an attribute; it cannot clear a pseudo-class, and pressing Escape does not move focus. Keyed on :focus-visible, the script wrote the panel shut and the panel stayed painted — the attribute said dismissed and the pixels said open, which fails 1.4.13 while every review of the source says it passes. Three files shipped that live, and one of them re-focused the trigger in its own Escape handler, guaranteeing the panel survived.
And a tooltip must never leave the accessibility tree. display:none and visibility:hidden strand every aria-describedby pointing at it, which turns this primitive from one that adds a reason into one that takes a reason away from exactly the keyboard and screen-reader users it exists for. Opacity is the ordinary way to satisfy that; the clipped visually-hidden pattern is the other one.

11 · Toast / transient outcome — <Toast>

AnatomyStack bottom-right, gap calc(var(--u)*.5). Each: title (--w-emph) + detail (--muted) + a dismiss control.
Surface--paper, 1px --rule-strong, plus a 2px --ink left edge — the emphasis rule, not a hue.
Motiontoast-in on --spring at --t-spring, rising calc(var(--u)*4).
ARIArole="status" — polite, because an outcome is not an interruption.
BehaviourThe terminal state of an asynchronous act. The bell is not this: a notification is for what happened while you were looking somewhere else.
ResponsiveNarrow: full width above the tab bar.
🛑 NeverA hue carrying success or failure — the words carry it · role="alert" for an ordinary outcome · a toast as the only record of what happened.

12 · Status bar — <StatusBar>

AnatomyThree slots, and they never rearrange: keys left · provenance middle · the audit trail last. 1px --rule-strong top edge.
ARIAcontentinfo.
BehaviourOne grammar on every protected surface, so a screenshot of any screen describes itself. The slot is never omitted: a surface with nothing to report says so in the slot where the report would have been — "no run for Aug 2026" rather than an empty middle.
ResponsiveNarrow: one tappable row that opens the full grammar as a sheet. Not truncated and not dropped — it moves. The row shows the provenance slot, because that is the half a person checks without being asked.
🛑 NeverReordered slots · an omitted slot · a truncated provenance line.

13 · Route-level states — <RouteState>

Kindsready · loading · empty · error. Each carries a scope and a time.
ARIAregion with aria-live="polite" on the state's heading, so a change of state is announced without stealing focus.
LoadingThe scope is stated while it loads, so a slow page is still a page about something. Skeleton rows are --sheet blocks; there is no progress bar, because that is a second line.
Four nothingsThey must not look alike: the filter's fault (clear-all is right there) · a clean night (a run that found nothing still reports) · no run at all (nothing has been measured, so there is nothing to be clean about) · waiting elsewhere (not an empty state — the work is moving, just not here).
ErrorNames what answered and what did not, and states that nothing has been changed — a failed read is not a failed run. Carries the audit id so the report is actionable.
ResponsiveNarrow: actions stack full width; the sentence stays inside --measure-act.
🛑 NeverA progress bar · a state without a scope or a time · an empty state that reads like a failure · a retry that does not say what it will retry.

14 · Key-caps and the shortcut legend — <Keys>

Anatomy.kb — mono --s1 at --w-figure, 1px inset edge, on --paper. .kb--on-ink inverts to a --on-ink-2 hairline.
UseOne way to state a key, at one size, in all four places it appears: a status bar, a menu row, a dialog and the palette footer. It replaces four ad-hoc mono spans at four different sizes.
ARIAkbd. ⚠️ A key-cap is not a target — it is a static label describing a keystroke, so 2.5.8 does not reach it and it is not padded to 24px. A clickable key-cap is a button wearing a cap, and it takes the floor.
ResponsiveNarrow: hidden, and the shortcut still works. A phone has no ⌘, so a cap naming one is an instruction a reader cannot follow — but a Bluetooth keyboard on a tablet can, which is why the shortcut is never removed, only its label. This is the one place the system hides information rather than moving it, and it is stated so it is not mistaken for the pattern.
🛑 NeverA shortcut that exists only as a cap · a cap at a size other than --s1 · a removed shortcut at the narrow tier.

🛑 320px is the floor, and it is a conformance requirement rather than a choice. 1.4.10 Reflow requires the whole interface to work at 320 CSS px with no two-dimensional scrolling. ⚠️ The breakpoints are constants, not usable tokens. A custom property cannot be read inside @media, so every breakpoint is still written literally at its site. They are declared so each number has one home and a fourth has to argue with them. Change the kernel first, then the copies. Adopting container queries would close this and is an open finding.
⚠️ Form controls are 16px minimum — max(1rem, var(--s2)). iOS Safari zooms the viewport when a focused input is under 16px, which breaks Reflow on every form in the system. The label above the field carries --s2; the field itself does not.

12 · Integration and theming

FileFormatFor
tokens.cssCSS custom propertiesThe authority. Must be loaded — the other three name the vocabulary, they do not carry the values.
tokens.tokens.jsonW3C Design Tokens (DTCG)The canonical interchange, carrying every token's one job as its description. What a design tool reads and what any future generator should consume.
tokens.sd.jsonStyle Dictionary sourceThe build input, for teams already emitting platform files.
tailwind.preset.jsTailwind theme presetWhat the React app imports. Class names are the token's job: text-refused, bg-sheet, border-rule-strong.
🛑 The Tailwind preset must never inline a hex. Every value is var(--token). A preset that copies #00303f into its palette forks the kernel: the dark theme stops applying, and there are suddenly two places a colour is declared. That is why tokens.css still has to be loaded.
🛑 And no build may flatten the dark theme into separate names. ink-dark / paper-dark breaks the rule that no component knows which theme it is in. Two themes over one set of names, switched by [data-theme="dark"] on the root element.

Three layers, and our vocabulary rather than atomic design's. The kernel owns every value and the rules cheaper to state once than to enforce per component. The fourteen primitives each own their states, their ARIA contract and their responsive behaviour; a primitive consumes tokens and composes other primitives, and never reaches into another primitive's internals. Compositions are the routes — they arrange primitives and own no visual decisions at all. A composition that needs a new visual treatment is asking for a primitive, and that is a design change.

The mapping onto atoms / molecules / organisms is close but not adopted: our primitives span the first two, because the useful line here is whether a thing has states of its own, not how many parts it has.

13 · Open findings

Each of these is a decision nobody has taken, recorded so it is not mistaken for an oversight — and so the next person does not have to re-derive it.

  • Container queriesThe three breakpoints are declared and unusable inside @media. Whether components should respond to their container rather than the viewport would change how every grid reflows. A design decision, not a value.
  • Interruptible motion--spring restarts rather than retargeting from its current velocity. Closing it needs a runtime, which is a stack decision rather than a token one.
  • forced-colors modeThe system has almost no transparency to reduce, but forced-colors would flatten the luminous touch and the hatch to nothing — and the not-built treatment would lose both its halves. A real gap.
  • No AAA positionThe target is AA and the system does not claim otherwise. Raising the ramp would collapse the distinction between --muted, --refused and --recede, which carry three different meanings.
  • 2.4.11 is asserted, not testedFocus Not Obscured depends on scroll behaviour that cannot be verified from a specimen sheet. It needs the real register at the real length, on a real keyboard.
  • No screen-reader transcriptThe ARIA contract is specified; what a given reader announces is a different claim. Three readers on three platforms is the test, and it has not been run.
  • The state label's markup<figure>/<figcaption> is the only form that associates a label with the thing it labels programmatically. The reference sheet uses it everywhere, which is a vote rather than a ruling.
  • The fourth groundThree grounds only, and a fourth is a finding. Nothing needs one — the entry tier came closest, wanting a --sheet page with a --paper card, and was refused because that makes --sheet a page ground under an existing name.
