Every surface, and the three records that govern them.

Open any file in a browser. Each one is self-contained apart from support.js, tokens.css and the stylesheet beside it, which must stay in this folder. Fonts load from the network, so the pack reads correctly online and stays legible offline.

Start with the three records. The surfaces are illustrative — every tenant, partner, figure and case in them is synthetic — and their job is to fix behaviour, not to be copied pixel for pixel.

What ships beside them

  • tokens.css — the kernel. Colour, type, space, layer, motion, the focus ring and the target floor. Everything else reads from it and no component may carry a literal value.
  • app.css — the protected shell: chrome, destinations, register, palette, tables, account menu.
  • public.css — the public plane and the error pages.
  • journeys.css · handoff.css · system-page.css — the three records. Not product code.
  • table.js — one table mechanism: sort and filter on the column header, text search, pagination. The count is computed from the rows that survived, a filter states what it removed, and sort is a view rather than a fact.
  • support.js — the runtime these files render through. Keep it beside them; a page without it renders blank.
  • doc-page.js — paged-document shell for the two printable records.
  • tokens.sd.json · tokens.tokens.json · tailwind.preset.js — the kernel exported three ways. Generated from the kernel, never hand-maintained: a hand-edited alias file drifts silently and shows up as one component that is nearly right.

Three things to know before estimating

  • The status words are the scope. built means drawn and operable in these files. specified means decided with no surface yet. partial names which half is missing. not built means knowingly absent — and where a surface shows that state at all it is drawn hatched with the reason attached, never as though it were handled.
  • The hatched blocks are the backlog. Every one of them names a thing that does not exist and why. They are the most useful pages in the pack for planning, and they are deliberately not sketches of a solution.
  • One disagreement is open and recorded rather than resolved. G36 slips bring-your-own-account to v1+1; the journey map keeps it in v1 by ruling. C12, P8, the partner sandbox and half the tenants in the staff console assume a customer-owned plane. Read the last section of the journey map before planning the deployment work.

Surfaces are designed at 1300px and above. Open at that width or wider. If a page appears blank, check that support.js is beside it in this folder.
