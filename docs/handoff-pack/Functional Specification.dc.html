How to read and update this

Every feature carries one of four status words, and they are not interchangeable.

  • builtDrawn on a surface in this project and operable there. A reader can click it.
  • specifiedDecided and written down — here or in the journeys — with no surface yet. Safe to build without a second conversation. A rule settled in review lands here, not in a status of its own: a decision with no surface is a specification.
  • partialSome of it is operable and a named part of it is not. The row says which part, because "partial" on its own is the status word that hides the most.
  • not builtKnown to be needed and knowingly absent. Where a surface shows the state at all, it is drawn hatched with the reason attached, never as though it were handled.

A feature moves status by being edited here in the same change that moves it on a surface. A status word that disagrees with the surface is the defect this document exists to prevent — so when the two differ, the surface wins and this file is wrong until corrected.

C9 · Configure sources and packs

The tenant administrator's surface, and the only one where a control's answer arrives from outside the product. Reached from Setup, which exists in the nav only for an admin.

Onboarding is an ordered sequence, and the order is load-bearing

Every step is blocked by the one above it. Nothing here is a preference; each gate exists because skipping it produces a period measured on documents nobody declared.

  • 1 · Declare the sourceKind, entity, and the connection its kind needs. The binding is ours; the credential inside it is theirs.
  • 2 · The test passes firstA source cannot be declared until its connection test passes. Declaring an untested source means nobody learns it is wrong until a run needs it.
  • 3 · A delivery arrives, and parksIt matches no pair yet, so it is parked as unclassified — the same bucket an unmatched pattern lands in. Nothing is extracted.
  • 4 · That parked document is the sampleThe first real delivery, held unextracted, is what the admin declares against — their paper, not a specimen of somebody else's.
  • 5 · Declare the pairSource × pattern → document type, plus the field mapping and the type's floor. Declared up front: nothing extracts until both exist.
  • 6 · The parked pile drainsAutomatically, on declaration, so the first period can close on documents that arrived before the pair existed. It is extraction, so the meter moves and the register says why.

What it refuses to do

The refusals are as much the product as the features are, and they are on the record rather than discovered.

  • No silent decisionsAnything below the floor is parked for a person, never guessed.
  • No partial registerA run that has read half the lines shows its progress, not half a picture.
  • No self-approvalUnless the tenant switched segregation off knowingly, and then every such approval is recorded as self-approved.
  • No hidden costEvery act that spends says so before it runs and prints what it spent after.
  • No credential readable from a requestWe custody each source credential in Secrets Manager under the tenant's own KMS key, and nothing that serves a request can read one back — which is also why some failures are indistinguishable from here.
  • No floor set from the close surfaceIt is an onboarding decision, and moving it mid-period would change what the register means.
